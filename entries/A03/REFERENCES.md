# Exact-value references for the original $I_{3322}$ functional

The coefficient table in this entry has classical bound zero. The preprints below give exact characterizations of the unrestricted value, with different model coverage. A supremum over paths is an exact variational definition. A normalizable strategy attaining that supremum is a further assertion. Each reference is annotated separately so that the model and the conclusion are explicit.

**J. Pauwels (2026).** *The quantum supremum of the $I_{3322}$ Bell inequality is not attained in finite dimension*. arXiv:2608.29734v1 [quant-ph], submitted 30 August 2026. Theorem 1 identifies the supremum over all finite local dimensions with the supremum of the Pál–Vértesi path family. Sections III–IV give the upper comparison and its realization by paths. Theorem 2 and Section V prove finite-dimensional nonattainment. Normal spatial attainment and whether the commuting-operator value exceeds the finite-dimensional supremum were left open in this paper. Appendix D describes the scope of its Lean formalization. [Version 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29734v1) · [Theorems and proof](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.29734v1#S2.SS3).

**A. Coladangelo (2026).** *The $I_{3322}$ Bell inequality requires infinite dimensions*. arXiv:2609.06038v2 [quant-ph], revised 5 October 2026. Equation (27) fixes the projection convention. Section 5 and Definition 5.4 specify the Jacobi matrices, the corresponding physical strategies and $q_*$. Section 7 proves the unrestricted spatial upper bound. Theorem 9.3 proves that no finite-dimensional strategy attains the value. Theorem 10.14 constructs a normal spatial attaining strategy; Theorem 11.1 combines the results. The accompanying entry uses the same Jacobi value and proves a commuting-model upper bound directly. [Version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06038v2) · [Final theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06038v2#S11).

**A. Krohn-Grimberghe (2026).** *Certified exact identification of the I3322 quantum value*. arXiv:2609.29693v1 [quant-ph]. Theorems A and B identify the common finite, spatial and commuting suprema with a unique zero of an analytic residual for a stable-curve boundary problem. Sections 8–10 treat dimension and attainment. Section 12 and Appendix C distinguish written analytic premises from checked finite calculations. The stable-curve identification is an alternative to the path definition used here. Its data deposit is part of the reference; no external computation from that deposit is required by this entry. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29693v1) · [Theorems A and B](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.29693v1#S1.SS1) · [Versioned verification deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22160086).

**V. Connor (2026).** *Maximal $I_{3322}$ Violation Requires Infinite Local Dimension*. Preprint, manuscript dated 1 August 2026. Definition II.1 and Theorem II.2 present an exact measure-variational characterization of the common finite-dimensional, normal spatial and commuting-operator suprema. Sections IV-VI give the universal upper bound; Sections VII-XI construct a normal spatial maximizing chain; Sections XII-XIV establish finite-dimensional nonattainment and approximation. Equation (II.1) uses the source $I_{3322}$ normalization specified in the accompanying proof. This is an alternative written proof, not a dependency of the supplied certificate. [Author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/411642957_Maximal_I_3322_Violation_Requires_Infinite_Local_Dimension).
