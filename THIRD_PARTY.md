The offline runtime is for 64-bit CPython 3.12 on Windows. It includes NumPy, SciPy, SymPy, mpmath, python-flint and threadpoolctl, with their applicable software licenses and component notices. NumPy 2.3.5 is a local LLVM-MinGW build with a documented binary64 ABI patch, not the official PyPI Windows wheel. Its NumPy, LLVM and MinGW notices are retained. The runtime parts reassemble to a ZIP, and the included license directories and Python metadata are readable after extraction. Original authors retain their rights; the project MIT license does not replace their licenses. No PowerShell/.NET distribution or MSVC runtime DLL is bundled. CPython 3.12 and 64-bit Windows, including their own platform runtime components, are separately installed prerequisites.

The complete distribution also supplies the corresponding sources, build recipes and additional component-specific notices described in [the runtime source-supply statement](third_party/runtime/SOURCE_SUPPLY.md).
