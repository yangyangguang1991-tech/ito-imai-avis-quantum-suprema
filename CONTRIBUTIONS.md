## Mathematical attribution

The authorship of this repository is distinct from the authorship of the
historical Bell inequalities, classifications, mathematical methods and
exact-value theorems that it contains. Those contributions retain their
attribution in the entries and historical references. A catalogue position
or a successful certificate replay is not a count of new theorems.

The material supplied by this edition includes uniform definitions and
normalizations, written upper-and-attainment arguments, exact certificate
data, verification implementations, coefficient correspondences and offline
reproduction instructions. These contribution categories must be distinguished
from prior discovery of the underlying exact value. A local reconstruction
using an earlier proof is not described as an independent discovery.

PROVENANCE.md and its associated machine-readable inventory identify the
mathematical sources and the origin of the supplied files. Exact-value
references, proof-method sources and software licenses serve different
purposes. A source used for an algorithm or construction is credited even if
it did not establish the corresponding unrestricted exact quantum value.
Absence of an identified earlier exact-value proof is not a priority claim.

## Computational assistance

AI-assisted tools were used during the predecessor research and preparation
of this edition, including exploration, drafting, program construction and
review. Human responsibility for the mathematical statements, references,
released files and corrections remains with the maintainer. Generated prose,
code, numerical agreement and automated reviews are not treated as proofs
by themselves; the proof obligations and reproducible exact checks are
specified in the mathematical materials.

The release is not a proof-assistant formalization of every included result.
External authors' descriptions of their own computational or AI assistance
are attributed to their publications in the historical notes. This repository
statement does not replace any more specific disclosure required by a future
journal, funder or institution.
