# Prior results and contributions of this edition

This repository preserves the historical A1–A89 catalogue. The inequalities and their numbering are due to Ito, Imai and Avis. Repository authorship does not claim their discovery, or ownership of the established methods and theorems used in the proofs.

## Established results and exact consequences

- **A1 (positivity):** the value zero follows from positivity of commuting effects and an elementary attaining assignment.
- **A2 (CHSH):** the exact value is a normalization of Cirel'son's sharp CHSH theorem.
- **A3 (I3322):** the exact characterization and attainment distinctions are credited to the cited recent authors. This edition supplies the path formulation and its reproduction materials. It does not claim a new I3322 value, algebraicity of the constant, or finite-dimensional attainment.

The general SOS, moment and Tsirelson/vector methods are established mathematical tools. In particular, **A8** is a correlation-only instance: this edition supplies its explicit five-square identity and attaining vectors. That instance computation is not presented as a new general Tsirelson theorem, and no worldwide priority claim is made for its radical value.

## Overlap with companion catalogues

The complete coefficients give **A1 = York Y1 = positivity**, **A2 = Y2 = CHSH**, **A3 = Y3 = I3322**, and **A4 = Y6 = I4322^(2)** (with party exchange and signed relabeling as documented). A1–A4 therefore also occur in the (6,3,2,2) catalogue. In addition, **A5 = Y51**, **A6 = Y9**, and **A7 = Y169 = I4422^(1)**. These identities preserve the full score, including constants and marginals. The repeated proofs and strategy transformations are included locally for reproducibility; duplicate catalogue positions are not additional discoveries.

## Earlier computational work

Pál–Vértesi and Hrga–Klep–Povh provide substantial earlier optimization results. The latter work reports numerical optimality and reconstruction results under its stated tolerances; these contributions must not be erased merely because this edition supplies exact arithmetic certificates. Its exact general extraction theorems and numerical instances have distinct roles.

In particular, **A51** already appears in their Table 3 with SDP/nonlinear-optimization agreement. **A60, A67, A80 and A87** appear in Table 4(a), and their discussion expressly reports numerical gap closure for **A80**. The present entry-specific contribution is the exact algebraic specification, universal upper certificate and attaining realization, rather than first discovery of a large numerical violation. Naceur–Wang–Magron–Acín subsequently apply rigorous rational outer-bound certification to A2–A89; outer-bound certification by itself does not establish matching exact optima throughout the catalogue.

## Materials supplied by this edition

Beyond the elementary/historically attributed A1–A3 and the locally transferred A4–A7, the edition supplies entry-specific exact proof materials for **A8–A13, A15–A20, A22–A46, A48–A61, A63, A65–A67, A69–A81, A83, A85–A88**. These include explicit exact defining data, dimension-independent upper arguments, attaining strategies, independent verification code and offline input binding. This is a statement of the supplied mathematical and software artifacts. It is not a claim that every listed exact value or proof idea is historically new. Any future priority claim requires a separate theorem-by-theorem argument and source comparison.
